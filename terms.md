# COA Reporting — Terms of Use

**Operated by Indeed Tech, LLC**

**Last updated: October 22, 2026**

## Purpose

COA Reporting is a private, internal reporting utility developed for Indeed Tech, LLC. It is designed to retrieve accounting information from the company's authorized QuickBooks Online account for internal reporting, reconciliation, and financial administration.

## Authorized Use

The application is intended exclusively for authorized representatives of Indeed Tech, LLC. It is not offered commercially, distributed to third parties, or intended for public use.

## QuickBooks Integration

The application accesses QuickBooks Online through Intuit's official API using authorized authentication. Users must have appropriate permissions to access the connected QuickBooks company.

## Limitations

The application is provided for internal administrative purposes. Reports should be reviewed for accuracy before being used to make financial or accounting decisions.

## Ownership

The application and its associated reporting processes are maintained by Indeed Tech, LLC.

## Contact

For questions regarding this application, contact Indeed Tech, LLC through its official business website.
