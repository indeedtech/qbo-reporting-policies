# COA Reporting — Privacy Policy

**Operated by Indeed Tech, LLC**

**Last updated: October 22, 2026**

## Overview

COA Reporting is an internal application used by Indeed Tech, LLC to retrieve and analyze information from its authorized QuickBooks Online company account.

## Information Accessed

The application may access accounting information through the QuickBooks Online API, including Chart of Accounts records, account classifications, account balances, account identifiers, and related financial reporting information.

## Use of Information

Retrieved information is used exclusively for Indeed Tech's internal accounting, reporting, reconciliation, and business administration.

## Data Storage and Security

The application is designed to generate reports for storage within Indeed Tech's controlled business environment.

OAuth credentials and authentication tokens are intended to be stored securely and excluded from public repositories and published reports.

Access to exported accounting information is restricted to authorized personnel under Indeed Tech's internal security practices.

## Information Sharing

Indeed Tech does not sell accounting information obtained through this application.

Information may be processed by Intuit as part of the QuickBooks Online integration. The application is not intended to transmit accounting records to unrelated third-party analytics or advertising services.

## Data Retention

Exported information is retained according to Indeed Tech's internal business and recordkeeping requirements and may be deleted when no longer needed, subject to applicable retention obligations.

## Access and Revocation

Authorized administrators can manage the application's QuickBooks connection and revoke authorization through applicable Intuit account controls.

## Policy Updates

This policy may be updated if the application's functionality, data handling, or security practices change.

## Contact

Questions regarding this policy may be directed to Indeed Tech, LLC through its official business website.
